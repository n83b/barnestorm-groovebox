Weekly Groovebox

Install to play

On mobile, Weekly Groovebox runs only as an installed app. This gives the instrument the full screen and keeps browser controls out of your performance.

  1. Tap Safari’s Share button.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Groovebox from its new icon.
  1. Open your browser menu.
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open Groovebox from its new icon.

↻ Turn your device sideways Weekly Groovebox is built for two-handed landscape play.
